# Admin guide

Sign in at https://risingwithrachel.com/admin. Use your email, your password, and the 6-digit code from the authenticator app. The code changes every 30 seconds. If you are signing in on a shared computer, use Sign out when you are done. The session ends when you close the browser tab.

## Inquiries

The Inquiries list is everyone who wrote through the site. Open a row to read the message. Create client saves that person as a client and links the inquiry. If the inquiry is already a client, the button opens the client instead. Reply by email opens your mail app.

## Clients

The Clients list is searchable by name, email, location, and notes. Open a client to edit notes and press Save notes. The inquiries that were linked to this client are listed under the notes.

## Sessions

On the client, choose a start time in Central time and a length of 30, 45, 60, or 90 minutes, then press Book session. The time has to be in the future, on a weekday, between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM Central, and it cannot overlap another session.

The client gets an email with a link to cancel or pick a new time, when email delivery is available. You can also press Cancel next to a session in this screen.

Until Google Calendar is connected, the session says it is waiting for Google Calendar credentials. After it is connected, a booked session is added to the calendar and a cancelled or moved session is updated there.

## Export

Export downloads a CSV of clients and sessions. Open it in a spreadsheet. It includes names, email addresses, notes, and session times.

## What is not on this screen

Payments are not shown yet. They stay off until the card processor is connected. You do not need to do anything with them.

If a client says they did not get the confirmation or a reminder, the message may not have been delivered yet. The session is still on the client profile. Ask the operator to check mail delivery before booking the session again.
